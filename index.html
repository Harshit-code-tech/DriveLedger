Features

Built around how you actually drive.

⛽

Multi-Fuel & EV Efficiency

Tank-to-tank calculations for Petrol, Diesel, CNG (km/kg), plus charge-to-charge efficiency for Electric Vehicles (km/kWh). Live estimates for partial fills and battery % calculation.

🔧

Record Maintenance

Track oil changes, brake pads, and garage bills. Kilometer countdown to your next scheduled service.

🚨

Tyre Pressure Card

Store factory PSI for front and rear tyres per vehicle. Check at petrol pumps right from your home screen.

🚗

Multi-Vehicle

Cars, bikes, scooters, and commercial vehicles. Instant switcher chips on every screen — no back-button navigation.

📄

Document Alerts

Store expiry dates for Insurance, PUC, RC, and Road Tax. Dashboard banner warns you before renewals lapse.

💾

AES-256-GCM Backup

Export your complete vehicle history and receipt attachments as a password-protected, encrypted .dlbak file anytime.

🛰️

Live GPS Cockpit & Trips

Offline digital speedometer, live distance, and route recording with automatic odometer sync and trip logs.

📱

3 Home-Screen Widgets

Quick Glance, live GPS Cockpit HUD, and minimal 1-row GPS Strip widgets right on your Android home screen.

🧮

Pay-by-Amount Fuel Entry

Just say "₹1,000 of petrol". Enter the amount paid or the litres — the other is calculated instantly, with quick-pick chips and your last price pre-filled.

🔋

Petrol, CNG, EV & Bi-Fuel

Per-fuel units and formulas — km/L, km/kg and km/kWh — with separate tracking for each tank on bi-fuel vehicles.

Install Guide

Three steps,
five minutes.

  1. 1

    Download the APK

    Click the button below to grab the latest DriveLedger.apk from GitHub Releases.

  2. 2

    Install on Android

    Open the file, tap Install. Android may ask to allow "unknown sources" — approve it for this once.

  3. 3

    Add your first vehicle

    The onboarding screen launches automatically. Add a nickname, fuel type, and registration number — you're in.

⚠️ When updating later:

Never uninstall before updating! Simply install the new APK over the existing app. Android replaces the code and keeps your entire database untouched.
